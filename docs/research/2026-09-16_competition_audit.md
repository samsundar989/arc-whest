## Competition contract that matters to the plan

Phase 2 evaluates fixed He-Gaussian, bias-free ReLU networks of width 1024 and depth 16, integrating over standard Gaussian inputs. The primary result is the mean across networks of final-layer error times max(0.1, FLOPs/2^41). All-layer error is diagnostic. Exceeding a hard limit causes a zero prediction without the discount. Runtime limits are 120 seconds total and 0.4 seconds residual per network, 5 seconds setup, and 8 GB for the participant process. The participant has one physical core; the backend/harness has seven more. These conditions motivate a separate deployment test from local accuracy scoring.

Ten submissions per team per UTC day are available. Submissions close October 17 at 23:59 UTC; team freeze is October 2 and contribution write-ups are due October 24. Prize ranking comes from fresh private re-evaluation of up to two nominated entries. Nomination instructions/deadline are to be published; do not infer them from a generic page's conflicting summary. The current rules are v14. Samuel's existing decision not to contact organizers is unchanged.

Sources: [overview](https://www.aicrowd.com/challenges/arc-white-box-estimation-challenge-2026), [rules v14](https://www.aicrowd.com/challenges/arc-white-box-estimation-challenge-2026/challenge_rules), [resources](https://www.aicrowd.com/challenges/arc-white-box-estimation-challenge-2026/dynamic_contents).

## Discussion findings and their limits

- **504aldo's factorized K3 release and September 13 reply:** strongest public implementation reference inspected. Their version ladder separates accuracy mechanisms from later cost reductions, and their negative results identify retained-history contractions as a bottleneck. They explicitly do not know the leaders' method. Their latest reply reports sponsor confirmation on September 11 that Strassen has no recursion-depth restriction; this is participant-reported correspondence, not a directly inspected organizer message. It also notes lower-cost derivatives on the board. Neither the dead-end table nor those derivatives establish an impossibility theorem or a winning architecture. [Thread and replies](https://discourse.aicrowd.com/t/everything-we-tried-a-factorized-k-3-cumulant-propagation-estimator-at-0-25-x-b-where-its-flops-go-and-25-measured-dead-ends-team-504aldo-rank-10/18218).
- **Local residual versus server behavior:** read all five posts, including corrections. Organizer confirms the then-live package versions; the participant subsequently identifies scalar materialization/graph flushes as the failure mechanism. Removing those synchronizations changed a failing entry into a clean entry without lowering the call count. Treat operation count as a proxy, and preserve a synchronization check and real server telemetry. The last post describes unresolved submission-level failures with large batched operands, not a proved memory diagnosis. [Timing thread](https://discourse.aicrowd.com/t/shipped-covariance-propagation-example-trips-the-phase-2-residual-cap-locally-0-16-0-but-grades-fine-what-happens-when-the-evaluator-upgrades/18202).
- **Phase 1 frontier corrections:** Cipo and thylinao distinguish small participation ratio from enough retained directions for accurate expectations. They also distinguish exact marginal readout from correct joint propagation. This is directly relevant to N1: low state rank or a good one-layer readout does not prove a good propagated estimator. These are Phase 1 observations, not numerical Phase 2 gates. [Discussion](https://discourse.aicrowd.com/t/phase-1-solution-ideas-for-phase-2-and-the-honest-public-frontier-all-methods-combined-and-explained/18193).
- **Zero fitted constants:** the author reports that calibration tracks accumulated propagation drift rather than the local Edgeworth residual. The disclosed 1.066e-7 adjusted result remains far worse than v31. Useful experiment-design lesson: measure the residual that the proposed correction actually consumes. It is not evidence that high-order cumulant propagation has hit its limit. [Write-up thread](https://discourse.aicrowd.com/t/phase-2-write-up-zero-fitted-constants-measured-what-the-64-calibration-numbers-in-a-relu-mlp-mean-propagator-are-actually-absorbing-submission-330001/18216).

Their off-suite smoke failure is a practical reminder to test generic shapes. [Thread](https://discourse.aicrowd.com/t/phase-2-write-up-exact-bivariate-relu-covariance-plus-per-layer-calibration-3x-over-the-kit-baseline-with-negative-results-submission-329951/18214).
