#### (a) The algorithm

**Backbone**: ARC's factorized K=3 "SIMPLE" cumulant propagation (Wu et al., arXiv:2605.05179 §S.4.3), ported to `flopscope` ops (`derivation_code_map.md:31`). The propagated state per layer is:

- `mu` (n), exact transport `mu <- W mu`;
- `C` (n×n) full covariance; `C_pre = W C W^T` (2 units; a "unit" = one n³ matmul = 2n³ = 2³¹ FLOPs, B = 1024 units — `derivation_code_map.md:14`); the **diagonal is exact** ("power cumulants"), off-diagonals are closure-approximated;
- the **third cumulant as a sum of hub sources**, one born per layer. A source is `T_s = Sym Σ_j X[:,j] ⊗ P[:,j] ⊗ Y[:,j]` with J = n columns (`derivation_code_map.md:34,60-66`). Only **two dense n×n legs per source are transported**: the propagator leg `P` (born as the identity, `P <- W d(w1) P`) and the birth leg `A = P a_b`, `a_b = d(w1) C_off`; `X = 3A` and `Y = A d(w2)` are column scalings, so they are never stored. The third leg `M` of the B3 sub-block is never transported: `M = P d(s) + 3 A d(e) + Z L^T` with `Z` a thin transported n×(R_RES+2) leg and `L` static (the V16b "7→4 restructure", F63; `derivation_code_map.md:38-39`).
- **The nonlinearity only reads two slices** of κ3: `D3_i = κ3_iii` and `D21_ic = κ3_iic` (oracle test F65: exact D3+D21 reproduces the exact chain). All of `_dslices` (`estimator_v29.py:1518`) collapses into two fused left factors contracted against `A^T` and `P^T`: `D21 = Σ_b LA_b A_b^T + LP_b P_b^T + O(n²r)` thin terms (`derivation_code_map.md:163-173`). Cost per (source, layer): 2 transports + 2 contractions = **4 units**, vs ~7 in ARC's reference.
- **κ4 is not carried; it is regenerated ("memoryless κ4 regeneration", V17, F68)**: the augmented chain's n×n matrix core is `G = diag(g) + λ_l · C_off` with **one scalar per layer** (R² 0.66–0.97 rising with depth). The diagonal `g` stays exact (`g_prev = cA (K4v + K22·1) + cI (ΣK4v + ΣK22·1)`, `cA = 6/(n+4)`, `cI = −3/((n+2)(n+4))` — the r=1 harmonic projection, `derivation_code_map.md:314-317`), transported as `dG = (W∘W) g + λ (diag(C_pre) − (W∘W) var)` (n²). Regenerating the diagonal too is 15× worse (F68). The gain comes through the **κ4→κ3 feed** into the newborn source (`X3 = A d(c1) + P d(c2)`, `Y3 = y 1^T`, `M_t1` rank-1 — `derivation_code_map.md:191-198, 292-298`); the use-side (3,1) terms alone are harmful (9.2e-8). The adaptive rule (V25): `λ_l <- LAM[l]·clip(ratio/REF_R[l], 0.5, 2)^BETA`, `ratio = mean(dG)/mean(var)` (`derivation_code_map.md:222-228`, `estimator_v29.py:310-334`).
- **D21 feedback into the births** (V18, F69): `Xt = 1.5 d(w2) D21`, `Yt = 0.5 d(w1) D21^T d(w3)`, compressed to rank R_FB = 16 by a randomized range finder whose sketch is `w32[:, :rfb]` (a slice of the layer weight, deterministic), carried as thin legs (`derivation_code_map.md:280-290`).
- **Final-layer trim** (V19, F71): the last layer needs only `var`, `D3` and the κ4 diagonal; no D21, no C_pre sandwich (exact, −8% cost). Dropping the last layer's source machinery entirely (D3 := 0) is 10× worse.
- **Age-gated shared basis** (V21/V22, F72) and **nested tier** (V24, F73): sources older than AGE_OLD = 4 transports are confined to one shared basis `Qc` (n×384), `A_s = Qc FA_s`, `P_s = Qc FP_s` with static factors; sources older than 7 move into a nested rank-224 sub-basis `U` (384×224). One source joins per layer via a one-pass randomized range finder on the weighted leg Gram (weights `dA = 9 + w2² + 9e²`, `dP = 1 + s²`; sketch = `w32[:, :r1]`) (`derivation_code_map.md:107-154`). Because the (2,1) contraction is a Hadamard product of legs, **old legs must be re-densified every layer** (`einsum("iq,kqn->kin", Qc, FAo)`), which is the "wall" of §5 of the post: a factored basis is a 2×-per-source lever, never 10×.
- **Term table**: the paper's diagram summation restricted to output partitions `{(1,),(2,),(3,),(4,),(1,1),(2,1),(2,2)}`, generated from ARC's `get_all_terms_iso` and **pruned at relative contribution ≥ 1e-5 (62 of 90 terms)** (`derivation_code_map.md:44`; `lean/dump_k3_tables2.py:1-20`), plus 4 `wk431` (3,1)-slice terms appended at import. Wick coefficients `w(k,p) = E[∂^k relu(Z)^p]` in a unified `σ^e (P1(α)φ + P2(α)Φ)` form.
- **What is dropped relative to ARC's SIMPLE/AUGMENT** (measured harmless on this base): the B2 sub-block (`shadow_B2`), the WK22 birth-factor term (`no_wk22_fac`), 28 of 90 term-table rows, the per-source κ4 legs of the augmented algorithm (replaced by the regeneration law), and the off-diagonal spill of `W diag(g − λ var) W^T` in the κ4 transport ("the free port", F68).
- **Cost engineering** (V26–V29, F79–F81): Strassen–Winograd block recursion written as ordinary flopscope ops, batched per "family" (all young legs of a layer as one `(k,2,n,n)` stack), 5 levels, min leaf 32, pooled `out=` buffers (`class _Strassen` at `estimator_v29.py:426`, `class _Pool` at `:627`; knobs `STRASSEN_LEVELS/HUB/SB/MIN/FIRST/FUSE_P` at `:415-422`). flopscope bills the recursion at its analytical count; MSE unchanged to 4 digits. The first `predict()` of a process runs one level shallower (`STRASSEN_FIRST = 4`) to dodge the pool-allocation residual spike. `predict` runs with `gc.disable()`. Block-symmetric `C_pre` as 3 of 4 block products; layer 0 uses the aliased Gram `w32^T w32` at 0.5 u.

**Fitted constants** (the complete list, `derivation_code_map.md:367-392`): `LAM` (16), `V17_LAM_SCALE = 0.95`, `REF_R` (15), `BETA = 1`, `R_RES = 16`, `R_FB = 16`, `AGE_OLD/R_OLD = 4/384`, `AGE_OLD2/R_OLD2 = 7/224`, `QPASS/QPASS2 = 1/2`. LAM/REF_R were least-squares-fitted on **8 public mini MLPs** (dumps 0–7) against ARC's dense augmented lean chain. Everything else is exact.

#### (a) The algorithm

`kprop_harmonic.mlp_kprop` (`src/mlp_kprop/kprop_harmonic.py:505-606`) alternates a **linear step** (`linear_kprop`, `:276-325`: each tracked cumulant `K[d]` is contracted with `W`; the "metric" `M` is updated as `W M W^T` or pinned to the average-case `init_scale·I` when `use_avg_metric=True`) with a **nonlinear step** (`nonlin_kprop`, `:328-462`): power cumulants `pK` are computed by a Wick/Edgeworth diagram summation around the matched Gaussian (`multiply_wicks`, `eval_part`), symmetrized, converted `pK → K` (`DS_pK_to_K`), and projected to the harmonic form `Sym(core ⊗ M^{⊗r})` (`DS_harmonic_proj`).

- **Harmonic decomposition** (`harmonic.py:13-25`): a degree-d cumulant is stored as `(core A, radial index r, metric M)` meaning `Sym(A ⊗ M^{⊗ r})`, i.e. the truncation to `⊕_{r'≥r} R^{r'} H_{d−2r'}`. "Harmonic" = decomposition of symmetric tensors into radial powers × harmonic (traceless) parts. `get_r_x` (`kprop_harmonic.py:194-230`) fixes which piece is kept:
  - `SIMPLE`: d ≤ k_max full (r=0); d = k_max+1 only if even, at r = d/2 (a **scalar** core: pure radial). So K3-SIMPLE carries κ4 as **one scalar**.
  - `AUGMENT`: d = k_max+1 at **r = 1** (an n×n matrix core `G` for κ4 in K3-AUGMENT); d = k_max+2 if even at r = d/2.
  - `BASE`: nothing above k_max (ablation only). `get_d_max` (`:233-245`): SIMPLE K3 → 4, AUGMENT K3 → 4, AUGMENT K4 → 6.
- **Diagram truncation** (`get_vec_cond`, `:51-64`): keep diagrams with `k(ν) = 1 + Σ_v cost(v) ≤ k_max`, `cost(v) = sum(v) − 1 − [all entries even]` (`vec_block_cost`, `:42-48`); the budget is `k_max − 1 + augment`, i.e. **AUGMENT raises the cap by one** and sums every diagram of squared size Ω(n^{−k_max}).
- **Factorization** (`factor_k3.py`, `factor_k4.py`): the top-degree cumulant is a `FactoredTensor` `Sym Σ_r A1[:,r] ⊗ A2[:,r] ⊗ A3[:,r]` (`:77-84`) whose factor count grows per layer; `contract_W` transports each leg (`:181-192`); the nonlinear step adds new factor columns from two-leg path diagrams (`:386-407`) and, in augment mode, from the κ4 (2,1,1) slice (`:409-453`). FactoredTensor4 is `Sym Σ_r A[:,:,r] ⊗ B[:,:,r]` (`factor_k4.py:73-76`).
- **Hypertree** (`is_hypertree`, `kprop_harmonic.py:67-83`): a diagram whose blocks form a Berge-acyclic hypergraph after merging parallel 2-support blocks; "Exactly these diagrams admit an O(n)-rank factored evaluation … e.g. covariance triangles and kappa_top x edge products do not."
- **`factored_keeps_term`** (`:86-101`): in augment mode the factored code drops (i) non-hypertree diagrams for the top slice `(1,)*k_max` and (ii) diagrams that pointwise-multiply the all-distinct block of the degree-k_max cumulant with other blocks. "In simple/base mode this keeps everything (the paper-basic term set is all-hypertree)."
- `metric_needed` (`:247-259`): SIMPLE with even k_max, and BASE, never consume the metric, so the `W M W^T` updates are skipped (bit-identical outputs, stale FLOP counts — see TODO).
- `symb/`: a tensor-network (whole-network contraction) version of the deprecated `kprop_ds` algorithm; "in practice, the constants are too large to be practical" (`symb/README.md`).
- FLOP model (`flop_utils.py`): torch `FlopCounterMode` with per-op models and a **fictitious symmetric-kernel discount** (`slice_factor`, `contract_factor`, `:412-437`) — the paper's counts assume symmetric-tensor savings flopscope does not grant (504aldo F5/F49: "NO symmetric discount; einsums charged at naive contraction cost").

### 2.2 The loop: idea → DEV score → FLOP/wall → submission

**Step 0 — build the candidate.** Candidates are single files in the repo root (`estimator_k3_v*.py`, 50–330 KB, stdlib + `flopscope` only). Several are generated: `tools/build_v57.py` inserts the corrector (weights as base64 float32) with `--verify` asserting bit-parity to v55 via `tools/_parity.py`; `tools/build_v60.py` the adaptive-age variant; `tools/variants.py` emits knob variants (`_CONST = value` rewrites) from an `arms.json`.

**Step 1 — screening (cupy, minutes).** `tools/probe.py <cand> --arms arms.json --panel dev` runs many module-constant arms on one imported copy on the GPU shim, paired on common nets, resumable; `tools/probe_fit.py` / `tools/fit.py` run CMA-ES on FIT minibatches (fit.py: 1.37 s/net on the GPU → "a 400-evaluation fit is an overnight job instead of a fortnight"). `tools/anatomy.py --dump` produces per-layer features for the corrector; `tools/corr_ceiling.py` (ridge) and `tools/corr_mlp_ceiling.py` (MLP) price a corrector before it is built; `tools/fit_corr_seq.py` does the sequential teacher-forced fit (136 layer-passes ≈ 8.5 full passes; 3h07m for 400 nets, `RESULTS.md:216`; 2h04m for the recursive variant). Heavy fits go through the machine's `workerq` (`AGENTS.md` Commands); evaluations do not.

**Step 2 — `tools/ev.py <cand> [--panel dev|fit|holdout] [--n 100] [--baseline <file>] [--mem] [--append]`** — the instrument of record (`tools/ev.py:1-52`). Two paths in separate processes:

- *Cost first* (`ev.py:572-581`, ~1 min): `tools/_cost_worker.py` runs ONE net with the genuine flopscope 0.12.1 under `local_sim/lifecycle_eval.evaluate`-style weights built as `f64_readonly` (float64, immutable — what the grader hands out). It reports FLOPs, op calls (sum of `summary_dict(by_namespace)` calls), scalar materializations (`p2_weff.MaterializationCounter`), setup time, and peak RSS. FLOPs are net-independent (spread 0 on every graded run) so one net is the whole cost answer. `local_sim` is APPENDED to `sys.path` so the real packages win.
- *Accuracy* (`ev.py:298-412`): `tools/_predict_worker.py` subprocesses with `local_sim` INSERTED at the front so `flopscope`/`whestbench` resolve to the non-counting numpy shims (`local_sim/flopscope/`, `local_sim/whestbench/`); 4 workers × 2 BLAS threads (more threads buy nothing: 50.02 s at 2 vs 50.05 s at 4 vs 73 s at 16); pool sized from free RAM using the cost path's measured peak RSS × `TRANSIENT_FACTOR = 2.5` (two 4-worker runs produced 51 and 60 MemoryError nets before this); `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1`; per-worker `.out/.err` files instead of pipes (a 64 KiB stderr pipe deadlock cost the evening of 2026-09-21). Weights are re-cast to float64 + `setflags(write=False)` so the candidate's opening cast takes the server branch. Predictions cached at `cache/preds/<sha256(source)>/<panel>/n<N>.npz` — never cached if any net failed. A failed net is stored as zeros, as the grader scores it.
- *Statistics* (`ev.py:479-503`): paired arithmetic score difference vs the baseline with a studentised-t 95% interval and wins/N (`competition_score.paired_diff_ci`); the geometric mean is printed as a diagnostic only. Score per net = `float32 MSE × max(0.1, F/2^41)` (`per_net_mse` mirrors `scoring.py`'s float32).
- *Projections*: `projected_public = mean(score)/PUBLIC_FACTOR`; server wall from the refit model; residual from ops.
- *Gate table* (`ev.py:531-563`, PLAN §5 as amended): DEV projected ≥3% better than best graded; ops ≤ 26,000; projected quiet-day wall < 100 s; setup < 2 s; materializations = 0; 0 failed nets; projected residual ≤ 0.33 s (D7 finals bound, not the 0.4 s cliff); optionally the WSL `RLIMIT_AS` 8 GiB gate (`--mem`, 3 predictions in one worker via `local_sim/linux_memory_gate.py`, with negative and positive controls). `--append` inserts the row above the marker in `RESULTS.md`.

**Timing per step** (measured, `RESULTS.md:236-241, 282-311`): cost worker ~1 min (a full twin `predict` is 70–95 s per net, `tools/cost_sweep.py`); numpy accuracy ~50 s/net → **38 min per 100 nets** with 4 workers (13 min with 6 was the PLAN estimate); cupy 2.03 s/net → **3.4 min per 100**; memory gate ~3 min; `whest validate` ~100 ms; `whest package` / `validate-package` seconds; server grading: 100 nets at 55–115 s each on the grader (all 100 walls are reported, so the run is at least ~1.5–3 h wall-clock; `tools/track.py` polls read-only because re-running `whest submit --watch` would consume a second daily slot). Board slots: 10/day, of which 5/day standing approval for canaries plus 1/day for an A/B designation-safety canary (`docs/HANDOFF_2026-09-24.md:3`).

**Step 3 — submission.** `uv run whest validate --estimator <cand>` → `whest package --estimator <release-dir> --output <pkg>.tar.gz --yes` → `whest validate-package` → `whest submit <pkg> --description "..." --watch` (guarded by `scripts/guard_paths.py`, which trips an "ask" on `whest submit`; under AGENTS rule 3 a gate-passing canary is pre-approved) → `tools/track.py <id> [--ledger]` → `local_sim/fetch_graded_report.py <id>` pulls the full per-MLP telemetry from the submission page (`flopscope_server_kernel_wall_s`, `_overhead_wall_s`, residual, failures). Submissions must be run from PowerShell or with `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8` (the CLI's box-drawing output kills a cp1252 console). `scripts/run_mini_json.ps1` exists because `whest run --format json > file` writes UTF-16 under PowerShell 5.1 and because the local residual cap fails every MLP locally (`--no-residual-wall-time-limit`).

### 2.4 The GPU shim and its agreement with real flopscope

`tools/gpu_shim/flopscope/` is a **non-counting** `flopscope` package backed by cupy (`__init__.py`: `BudgetContext` is a no-op whose `__getattr__` raises so nothing can read a FLOP from it; `as_symmetric` is the identity; `numpy.py` star-imports cupy, drops numpy's `optimize=` kwarg on `einsum`, exports `asnumpy`; `stats.py` maps `norm.cdf/pdf/ppf` to `cupyx.scipy.special.ndtr/ndtri`). Selected by `WHEST_BACKEND=cupy` in `_predict_worker.py:39-48`, which inserts it ahead of `local_sim`. It runs on the RTX 5090 at **2.03 s/net** vs ~50 s CPU (host RSS 8.0 GiB; do not run both backends at once).

**Agreement is with the numpy accuracy path, not with flopscope's FLOP count (it has none).** `tools/check_backends.py` measures it: both run float32 and the GPU reduces in a different order, so sixteen layers amplify associativity into **~1e-3 relative on a per-net MSE** (worst net 1.9e-3); the per-net errors are signed and roughly independent, so the **100-net panel mean differs by +3.2e-05** (C10: cupy 3.095064e-08 vs official `whest run` 3.094966e-08; `RESULTS.md:236-261`). It is not TF32 — cupy's 1024³ f32 product is *more* accurate than the CPU BLAS against f64 (3.5e-7 vs 7.2e-7). Policy: cupy is a **screening instrument** (probes, fits, anatomy, parity); numpy is the **instrument of record** (every `RESULTS.md` row and §5 gate), and numpy reproduces the official `whest run` raw MSE to every digit it prints. The real flopscope run (cost path) agrees with the server FLOP count exactly (above), and its wall is only used through the fitted model.

Note on "the shim reads ~18% low" (`AGENTS.md` Measurement, `PLAN.md:53`, `ev.py:20`): the numpy shim does not count FLOPs at all; the 18% is a Phase-1 finding that `local_sim/eval_estimator.py`'s **MSE** read 18% low for rotation-using estimators (`GOTCHAS.md:1804`, `SKEPTIC.md:88-90`). The sentence survives as folklore; the operative rule (take FLOPs only from `_cost_worker.py`) is right for a different reason.

### 2.6 Known bottlenecks in the loop

1. **The numpy accuracy path is 38 min per 100 nets and RAM-bound, not core-bound** (~3.8–5.2 GiB per worker, ×2.5 transient, on ~30 GiB free; `ev.py:196-206, 221-234`). Every `RESULTS.md` row costs that. cupy is 11× faster but is not the instrument of record.
2. **A submission costs ~2–3 h of grader time plus a scarce slot** (10/day, 5 canaries + 1 A/B), and the score gate is now binding against the project's own best (`STRATEGIST_PACKET_2026-09-25.md` §3-Q1: v59 at 4.9763e-9 is 1.35% better and cannot be sent).
3. **Server wall is the constraint, not FLOPs**: leaf-32 Strassen chains run ~1.9× the ps/FLOP of leaf-64 chains, and a noisy day adds a ×1.24 tail; every "noisy" reading held is a projection. One failed net (>120 s or >0.4 s residual) scores ~0.9 vs 5e-9.
4. **Op count governs residual and memory**: Strassen glue is 63% of the op budget (8,724 → 23,421 ops with Strassen on; `STRATEGIST_PACKET_2026-09-22.md:112-119`); pooled `out=` buffers are impossible on the grader's immutable arrays, so 504aldo's F80 residual engineering does not transfer.
5. **Cost meter is one net per candidate** (fine while FLOPs are net-independent; `tools/cost_sweep.py` exists for adaptive-cost candidates at ~90 s/net/variant).
6. **Operational traps that already cost time**: pipe deadlock in the worker pool (fixed), HF hub rate-limiting without `HF_HUB_OFFLINE` (fixed), UTF-16 JSON from PowerShell redirects, `np.load` handles blocking unlink on Windows, `sys.path.insert` of `local_sim` making counted FLOPs fiction, `linux_memory_gate --repeats < 3` silently reporting UNAVAILABLE.
7. **Documentation drift** that a new session could trip on: `ev.py` and `AGENTS.md` still print the 9e-11 noise floor; `AGENTS.md`/`RESULTS.md:18` still quote the `×1.3097`/`×1.5981` wall factors; `PROVENANCE_504aldo.md`'s P3/P4/P5 rows are behind the code; the "shim reads 18% low" sentence is a Phase-1 MSE artefact.
8. **Fitting throughput**: the sequential corrector fit is ~3 h per 400 nets on the GPU queue; CMA-ES fits on the chain plateaued at 2.7% (`STRATEGIST_PACKET_2026-09-22.md:100-106`).
