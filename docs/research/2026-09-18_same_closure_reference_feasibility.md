## Cost and call envelope

The C10 source's `_projected_flops` is a deliberately conservative **budget guard**, not a counted-cost report. At `n=1024`, `depth=16`, `gens=None`, its own formulas give 467 dense-equivalent matmuls; with C10's 27 bivariate closure rows, the P2-full (`r=1024`) base and thin allowances sum to about **2.256 trillion** FLOPs, versus **1.069 trillion** at `r=32`. The true C10 f64-readonly meter is only **480.851 billion**, showing why neither guard figure is a forecast. The full-rank guard is already above `2**41` and the normal 0.95 budget threshold, so calling normal `Estimator.predict` may generation-cap or fall back and invalidate the reference. An offline diagnostic runner must call the same `_layer` with `gens=None` under a sufficiently large instrumented budget, recording every operation; it must assert all 15 births and no truncation. Never export the budget-bypass as a grader candidate.

For an independent arithmetic envelope, replacing `r=32` with `r=1024` changes the guard's four carry/reconstruction terms by about **1.07 trillion** FLOPs over the 16-layer run and the birth range-finder/QR allowance by about **0.12 trillion**. This is a deliberately loose upper-style planning increment because C10's Strassen and block-symmetric paths make the source guard pessimistic. Adding it to the metered C10 total gives a rough **1.67 trillion** before the unshared-history policy's effect; it is *not* a measured complete cost or proof of fitting under `2**41`. The no-share arm removes join QR/projections but expands old-history contraction widths, so its signed complete cost must be metered, not inferred from the dense sentinel's 621.352 billion (different settings). P2 full also introduces 30 full-size QR calls across 15 births; full-width operation calls and residual time are likely less favorable than FLOPs suggest. Collect exact counted FLOPs, operation calls, scalar materializations and peak process memory for all three arms on the same canonical one-network pilot before a panel. A reference above the score budget can still answer an offline accuracy question; its effective adjusted score is not a valid deployable result.
