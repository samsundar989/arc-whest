**Strongest challenge.** The work can be technically correct while its next-step interpretation is too restrictive: matching a numerically unstable float32 reference is not itself evidence of better expected-activation accuracy. Conversely, a 1% prediction-drift-energy allowance is not a 1% score-effect guarantee. Neither issue invalidates the recorded failed gate; both matter when choosing the next scientific contract.

## Static review of the two proposed successors

**Verdict: ACCEPT as concrete proposals with the scope below.** Reviewed `2026-09-14_e14_math_review.md` and `2026-09-14_e14_accuracy_review.md` against the frozen F21 `_advance`, `_wick_tower`, `_dslice3`, `_dslice21`, `_append`, and `_layer` bodies. No new numerical experiment was performed.

For the mixed residual kernel, substituting `A=Q F_A` and `P=Q F_P` in the three frozen D21 products gives the proposed `LA`, `LP`, shared Q return, and separate `(P*P)@D.T/3` return. The coefficient 2/3 in LP and coefficient 1/3 on the separate return are correct. Retaining full f32 D while advancing Q and reconstructing A/P in f64 is a specified new arithmetic policy; casting PP to f32 before its residual return is essential to the claimed f32 tariff. Full-rank Q preserves the real tensor and need not meet the compressed cost target. The hypothetical lower-rank costs do not choose a rank.

I independently recomputed the entire displayed mixed-kernel product/QR table as scalar integer arithmetic. The source's exact recursive tariff is `S4(1024)=1,366,126,592`; tower products removed from the F21 ledger total `880,957,820,928`; the conservative retained backbone is `47,499,816,550`. The four totals, including the stated extra envelope, independently reproduce `541,948,967,886`, `615,258,560,822`, `690,954,856,038`, and `769,061,184,462` for hypothetical r=32,64,96,128. The installed `qr_cost` supports the displayed explicit-Q f64 charge. The join accounts for H construction, QR, old overlap/rotations, new projections and Gram maintenance. The extra scalar envelope is a generous implementation allocation, not an executed ledger or proof about arbitrary implementations. Runtime, allocator behavior and process memory are still open. In particular, the r=128 upper envelope exceeding a ceiling cannot reject every implementation at that rank.

For the scalar-metric feed identity, `Q_iijk=m G_jk/6` for distinct i,j,k and the quadratic Taylor coefficient a_i/2 give m/12, not m/6. The three U terms therefore match the full J on all-distinct entries. The repository's repeated-slice reconstruction is supported only on repeated entries and is identity on that subspace; hence `J-D(J)=Jred-D(Jred)`. This is an identity of the complete tensor, conditional on the declared finite response, not merely of two readouts. The later linear transport is consistent with that identity. It does not establish that scalar-metric K4 or the covariance-aligned approximation is accurate.

The shared-U D21 formula is also correctly normalized: `U_iij=2*y_i*S_ij+y_j*S_ii`, so the symmetrized X gives `y_i*(X_ij+X_ji)+t_i*y_j`. Each additional fused operand has factor 3 because F21 divides the combined numerator by 3. The extra outer(t,y) supplies the remaining term and cannot be dropped. Moving w2b from F21's third right operand to the third left operand is a real-arithmetic identity but a new rounding order. An active fused-versus-active-unfused numerical comparison and direct frozen call when disabled are necessary. No missing factor of 3 or square product was found in those equations.

Two requirements should be explicit before executable implementation:

- Price the new active program's generic-shape and budget fallbacks. The old F21 chooser was priced for the old program; preserving its code does not by itself preserve safety after adding work. Any generation truncation must apply to extra y/r state and represented factors in lockstep with their source ages. This is separate from the promised full-age live-shape experiment.
- If the arbitrary-G dense-S variant is later selected, define floating-point symmetrization after `A S A.T` or at its U consumers and charge it. Exact algebra preserves symmetry; independently rounded products need not. The proposed aligned shared-U implementation already specifies `(X+X.T)/2` and avoids this ambiguity.

The identity-only accuracy task can use supplied G/covariance/derivative fixtures and proceed independently of QR without selecting regeneration. A later aligned-tree candidate is a new closure intervention and needs its own strategic contract. Its square-product arithmetic is explicitly incomplete; only the full counted candidate ledger can establish price. No new score, accuracy benefit, admissibility, rank adequacy, or deployment pass follows from these accepted proposals.
