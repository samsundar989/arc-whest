## Slice B — exact scalar-metric feed identity and factor sharing

**First stage: supplied-core mathematical test, independent of Slice A.** Record an IMPORTANT prediction. Do not choose or evaluate the regeneration rule, fit a coefficient or load labels yet.

`J-D(J) = U((m/12)*a, diag(b)Gdiag(b)) - D(U((m/12)*a, diag(b)Gdiag(b)))`.

Use explicit signed symmetric G, unequal derivatives, diagonal G, the width-three normalization witness, and nontrivial later linear/Wick maps. Dense enumeration must not reuse the reduced formula. Compare the **full next K3 tensor**, including every repeated-birth correction, then two subsequent nonlinear layers under identical supplied closure states. This establishes persistence, not merely immediate readout equality.

Then compare fused and unfused active queries in the actual declared precision. Fusion moves w2 across F21's third product and is not a bit identity. Disabled-extra mode must call the unchanged frozen path. Preserve existing gates; any different numerical regime requires its own prospective contract. No metric, rank, covariance repair or generation policy may change to hide a failure.

**Stop conditions:** failed full-tensor cancellation; a needed new dense factor omitted from the sharing proof; or unresolved active-query numerical failure. Do not spend a decision block to debug these issues.
