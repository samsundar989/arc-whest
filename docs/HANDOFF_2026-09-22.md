## The mechanism, in the code's own terms

In `estimator_k3_v53_age5.py`: an exact lineage costs 5 matmul-units/layer — `_advance` transports two dense legs (`W^T A_L`, `W^T M`), `_dslice21` is three `(n,kn)@(kn,n)` products, `_dslice3` is elementwise. The leaders spend ~1.1 units/source-layer all-in. The D-block alone, `A_D = M·d(sb)`, is single-leg: transport = 1 product, D21 = `(M∘M·d(sb)) Mᵀ` = 1 product, D3 = `Σ_j sb_j M_ij³` = free, and the diagonal-born κ4/κ5 ride on the same `M` for free. The correlation-born block (`A_L = w1∘C_off`, the `3A_L·d(eb)` term, the `Z Lᵀ` residual) is what costs the second leg and two of the three D21 products. Hypothesis: carry the D-block exactly for every source at age ∞ (no compression, ever), regenerate the correlation-born κ3 memoryless from the current `C_off` with a fitted per-layer scalar, the way κ4 already uses `λ·C_off`.
