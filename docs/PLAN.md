## 2. Architecture verdict

**Family: right.** Every top-10 entry has deterministic-chain telemetry; the organizers named this family as the winning one; sampling is measured dead at this shape by us and by others. **Design: at its measured local optimum.** Age, rank, tiers, single-leg, N1, adaptive age/λ, deeper Strassen, K4, P3, closure tail, joint refit, hidden-64, recursive feature — all measured and closed (evidence pack B). What separates 2.0e-9 from 5.0e-9 is therefore (i) a representation of old κ3 content we have not found, and (ii) accuracy sources we have not built. This plan spends the first week on (ii), because it has the most evidence behind it and the tooling to test it exists, and treats (i) as a second lane only after a diagnostic (§3, Step 1) tells us where the error actually is.

**The Sep 25 packet's "no untried accuracy lever left in the spec" is true of the spec, not of the evidence.** Never built, each with a primary source:
- **End-to-end fitting through the chain** (organizers: "learned networks that consume the cumulant estimates as features"; jamesrahenry /t/18097: "step-local accuracy does not compose in an error-compensating dynamical system" — his DAgger-style sequential fit was 8.4× on a K2 chain and a pooled fit "diverges within two iterations"; our own anatomy: 79% of final error is propagated drift; our greedy corrector −7%, naive −0%).
- **Channel-level drift diagnosis with exact pre-activation labels** (keenanpepper `arc-whestbench-p2-full1000-N1e9`: dense `pre_M11 = E[z_i z_j]`, `pre_M21 = E[z_i² z_j]`, `pre_M31 = E[z_i³ z_j]`, `pre_M22 = E[z_i² z_j²]` and marginal moments to 4th order, all 16 layers, all 1000 full nets, N = 1e9, 337 MB/net; `whest-p2-bakev2-mini-supp`: pre/post central moments to 6th order + `gate_p` + dense post `K21/K22` for the 100 mini nets = our DEV panel). AndreasHad04 (/t/18218, Sep 22) measured that 504aldo's `G_off = λ·C_off` has R² 0.998 → 0.22 (L8) → 0.045 (L15) against the true κ4 (2,2) slice; 504aldo replied his fit is against a different object (r=1 matrix core). Nobody has measured *our* chain's per-channel error against the truth.
- **Multi-mode κ4 core** (Puffi's SSC: κ4 core = Σ_ab Λ_ab B_a⊗B_b with ≤4 covariance-response modes, ridge-fitted Λ per layer; −77% vs factorized K3 at Phase-1 shape; refitting Λ to the *consumed mean correction* gave a further −13.6%). Our λ·C_off is the one-mode case.
- **A σ/covariance-channel corrector** (our one-step residual concentrates at α ≈ 0, where ∂E[ReLU]/∂σ = φ(0) dominates and mean-only correction can only patch after the fact).
- **Harmonic projection-at-fold old tier** (keep the non-traceless part Sym(u ⊗ C) of a folded lineage at n² cost; the T3 experiment tested memoryless *regeneration*, not projection; the ARC townhall's "effective rank falls with depth" hint).
- **Wall engineering** so deeper Strassen becomes designable (alternative-basis Strassen–Winograd, 12 adds/level; the 15-add form; a no-`concatenate` block layout; fewer larger calls). J2W runs 340 ps/FLOP at 0.127 B inside 117 s; we run 171 ps/FLOP at leaf 32 and cannot afford leaf 16.

### Step 1 — channel-drift anatomy with exact labels (Sep 25–26, ≤ 1.5 days, before any fitting)
Build `tools/anatomy2.py` on the cupy probe with hooks on the pre-activation state of v59 at every layer. Against the labels, per layer l = 2..16 and per DEV net (start with 20 nets, extend to 100 with bakev2):
1. **Object errors:** relative Frobenius error of `mu_pre`, `var_pre` (diag), `cov_pre` off-diagonal (split trace-part vs traceless-part), `D3 = κ3_iii`, `D21 = κ3_iij` (central, from `pre_M21`), `D4`, `Q31 = κ4_iiij` (from `pre_M31`), `D22 = κ4_iijj` (from `pre_M22`). For D21 also report the error of the **T0 unshared chain** on the same nets, so compression error and closure/birth error are separated.
2. **κ4 model check:** per layer, the best scalar λ* against the true off-diagonal κ4_iijj, its R²; and the R² of a 4-mode model {C_off, C_off∘C_off, ((g_i+g_j)/2)·C_off with g = gate probability, μ_iμ_j·(1−I)} fitted by ridge on FIT nets and evaluated on DEV. This prices Step 2 stage 3 before it is built.
3. **Truth-substitution one-step test** (the decisive table): for each layer, run one chain step from the true labelled state with **one object at a time replaced by its truth** (covariance; D21; D3; the κ4 slices; all of them) and report the one-step mean MSE at layer l vs the labelled μ_l, next to the unmodified one-step MSE. The share of one-step error recovered by each substitution ranks the channels. Then a **rolled** version: substitute the same object at every layer ≤ l and read the accumulated MSE at 16 — this measures how much of the 79% propagated drift each channel owns.
Decision: the channel(s) recovering the largest share become Step 2's fitting targets and Step 3's closure change. Predictions to record before the run — strategist: covariance substitution recovers < 15% of one-step error; D21 30–50%; κ4 slices 20–40%; λ·C_off R² on κ4_iijj < 0.5 by layer 10 while the 4-mode model stays > 0.8; old-vs-young split of the D21 error [?]. Worker: record own.

### Step 2 — differentiable twin and end-to-end fitting (Sep 26–30, the main bet)
Build `tools/twin/` : a PyTorch float32 CUDA re-implementation of v59's forward chain **without Strassen or flopscope plumbing** (plain `matmul`; same math, same tables, same constants; `torch.linalg.qr` for the range finder). Acceptance: matches the cupy probe to ≤ 1e-4 relative on per-net final MSE on 3 nets, and reproduces v59's DEV raw to 0.1%. Memory: checkpoint per layer (`torch.utils.checkpoint`); one net per step with gradient accumulation; expect ~6 s/net/step on the 5090 [I].
Parameters (all embedded as constants at inference; FLOPs unchanged unless stated): (i) corrector pages, initialised from v59; (ii) λ, θ, feed profiles as free per-layer scalars initialised from the current splines; (iii) per-term-class gains on the closure table (25 terms + 30 extras, per layer); (iv) **multi-mode κ4 core** Λ_l,a for the 4 modes of Step 1.2 (elementwise n², ≤ +1% FLOPs); (v) **σ-channel corrector**: a second 33→32→1 page per layer outputting a multiplicative correction to `var_pre` (or to `diag(cov_out)`), applied to the state — only if Step 1 implicates variance.
Objective: mean final-layer MSE over training nets (+ 0.1 × all-layer MSE as a stabiliser, ablate once). Panels: TRAIN = FIT 100 + HOLDOUT-300 + 500 of HOLDOUT-600 (record ids); HOLDOUT-100 = the remaining 100, read once per stage; DEV 100 untouched for selection; optional extra corpus: the 2,048 d8b nets' `final_mean` (N = 1e8, noise ≈ 9e-10 ≈ 4% of the signal) as a second epoch source. Warm start reproduces v59 at step 0. Stages, each ending with a DEV read and a HOLDOUT-100 read: **S1** corrector end-to-end; **S2** + closure/κ4 profiles; **S3** + multi-mode κ4; **S4** + σ corrector. Export via `tools/build_twin.py` → bit-parity of the flopscope chain against the twin to 1e-5 on 2 nets → f64 cost run → canary any stage whose projected score beats the best graded (decision 1).
Predictions (strategist, discounted for my optimistic record): S1 −3 to −6% raw over v59; S2–S4 a further −5 to −15%; target raw ≤ 2.0e-8 ⇒ ≈4.3e-9 at 0.216 B. Worker: record own. Kill/fallback: twin not matching to 1e-4 within 2 days ⇒ CMA-ES on the same parameter set via the cupy probe (S2–S3 only, overnight); S1 < 1% over greedy on DEV ⇒ skip to S2–S3; HOLDOUT-100 disagreeing with DEV by > 1.5 pp ⇒ reduce parameters (shared pages with a layer embedding) before shipping.
